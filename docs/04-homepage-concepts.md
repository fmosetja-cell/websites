# Five Homepage Design Concepts

All five share the same content hierarchy, firm facts, and brand palette (warm beige/champagne, charcoal, black, silver-grey, white, muted bronze accent). They differ in composition, type treatment, and how strongly they foreground the AI/technology story. None uses gavels, scales-of-justice icons, or stock courthouse photography — line-drawn architectural motifs, document/grid motifs, and typographic composition carry the "legal" register instead.

## Concept 1 — Institutional Authority (`homepage-1-institutional.html`)
A formal, structured layout built on a strict grid, evoking a public-sector-facing institution. Charcoal and beige dominate; bronze is used sparingly for dividers and icons. Hero uses a full-width architectural line-pattern panel rather than a photograph. Best suited if the firm wants to lead with its municipal/public-sector and forensic credibility.

## Concept 2 — Modern Approachable (`homepage-2-approachable.html`)
Warmer, softer composition: rounded card corners, generous whitespace, friendly iconography for the value pillars and practice areas. Serif headlines paired with a humanist sans body. This is the recommended default for the inner-page mockups in this phase, since it balances premium tone with approachability for individual clients (personal injury, labour matters) as well as institutions.

## Concept 3 — Tech-Forward / AI-Enabled (`homepage-3-tech-forward.html`)
Leads with the AI Legal Assistant and Client Portal as the hero's visual centrepiece (a stylised chat/dashboard preview panel sits beside the headline instead of below the fold). Darker charcoal hero, bronze accent glow on interactive elements, monospace micro-labels on data-style components (reference numbers, status chips) to signal "systemised" without looking like generic SaaS.

## Concept 4 — Editorial / Document-Inspired (`homepage-4-editorial.html`)
Uses a legal-document/paper motif: faint ruled lines, margin numerals, a pulled-quote treatment for the director's statement, and a magazine-style asymmetric grid. Feels the most "law firm as trusted institution with a point of view" — closest to a premium professional-services publication.

## Concept 5 — Minimalist Premium (`homepage-5-minimalist.html`)
The most restrained: almost entirely charcoal/white/bronze, thin-weight serif display type at large scale, one strong statement per section, minimal iconography, no card shadows. Reads as the most "premium boutique" of the five, at some cost to information density — best suited if the firm wants to prioritise brand feel over surfacing every service on the homepage.

## Recommendation
Concept 2 (Modern Approachable) is recommended as the safest default across both institutional and individual audiences, and is the concept used to build out the eight inner-page mockups in this phase. Concept 3 is the strongest alternative if the firm wants the AI positioning to be the headline story rather than a supporting feature.
