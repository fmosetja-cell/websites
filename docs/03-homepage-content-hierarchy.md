# Homepage Content Hierarchy

1. **Utility bar** — phone (click-to-call), email, office hours, Client Portal login link.
2. **Primary navigation** — logo (placeholder wordmark, see content gaps), main nav, mega menu for Practice Areas, prominent "Speak to an Attorney" button.
3. **Hero** — headline "Experienced Legal Counsel. Strategic Solutions. Trusted Representation.", supporting line, primary CTAs (Speak to an Attorney / Start a Secure Enquiry), secondary CTAs (Explore Our Services / Correspondent Instructions).
4. **Firm introduction** — since-2014 positioning, four value pillars (client-centred, ethical & dependable, technology-enabled, institutionally trusted).
5. **Key practice areas** — 7-item grid linking into the mega-menu structure.
6. **Why choose Mokhetle Attorneys Inc.** — differentiators drawn from the strategy doc (breadth, institutional trust, geographic advantage, technology).
7. **Director profile** — Edgeworth Mokhetle summary + link to full profile.
8. **Institutional & public-sector experience** — category-based "Selected Experience" (no unconfirmed client logos).
9. **Forensic investigation capabilities** — capability checklist matching the brief.
10. **Correspondent attorney services** — proximity to the North West Division of the High Court, services offered to instructing attorneys.
11. **AI-assisted enquiry feature** — Mokhetle Legal Assistant preview + disclaimer + link through to the full demo.
12. **Client portal preview** — static dashboard mockup teaser + "coming soon" framing, since the portal is not yet live.
13. **FAQ** — accordion, sourced from brief-consistent, non-committal answers (no compensation estimates, no outcome guarantees).
14. **Contact & location** — address, phone, email, hours, map placeholder, enquiry form.
15. **Final call to action** — repeat of primary CTAs before the footer.
16. **Footer** — sitemap, legal/POPIA links, registration number, social/WhatsApp, sticky call/WhatsApp buttons persist across scroll.
